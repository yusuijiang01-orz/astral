# V2-B — Astral Forest Ruins, first authoring pass

Status: IN_PROGRESS / ART_REVIEW_PENDING. Entered after V2-A software acceptance, not after production character delivery.

Default entry loads the new environment review. Three camera buttons and orbit inspection are available. Renderer QA remains `?qa=1`; old gameplay is opt-in `?debugAssets=1` only.

Original source assets, CC0: `src/world/forest/geometry.js`, `AstralForestRuins.js`, generated PBR maps in `public/assets/forest`. These are production-directed authoring, not reused QA GLB or old prototype assets. Geometry is custom sampled surfaces: height field, swept branched trunks/roots, leaf blades, arch bands and weathered stone surfaces. No BoxGeometry, ConeGeometry or old WorldView in this environment.

Implemented: sculpted terrain, central elliptical courtyard, curved approach, flank spaces, three broken astronomical gates, perimeter masonry, branched trees and roots, leaf crowns, undergrowth, boulders, crystal landmarks, distant ridges, warm/cool LightingRig, dynamic shadows, fog and selective bloom. QualityManager reduces undergrowth density. Three software screenshots are generated by Actions.

Not complete: dedicated bark/leaf material art, detailed wall carvings, authored architectural stair set, unique sculptures, crates/barrels/braziers, better foliage LOD and instancing, environmental GLB export, collision/navigation and encounter integration. Current trees and stones require further art refinement. No production player/monster added. No claim of final asset approval or target mobile performance.

Production Character Gate remains V2-C. The QA GLB never appears in this environment review.
